## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an ultra-luxurious, digital flagship presence calibrated for connoisseurs of rare estate coffees, barrel-aged infusions, and artisanal roastery experiences. It treats coffee cultivation, micro-lot sourcing, and molecular brewing as high craft—evoking the hallowed, sensory ambiance of a Milanese tasting room or an architectural Tokyo reserve atelier.

The visual style is an opulent synthesis of **Tactile Editorial Luxury** and **Dark Glassmorphism**:
- **Tactile Materiality**: Surfaces feel physically milled, utilizing brushed metallic accents, patinated copper trims, and fine hairline rules reminiscent of vintage brass roasting machinery and bespoke letterpress tasting cards.
- **Dark Glassmorphism**: Layers of ultra-dark espresso tones hover over ambient emerald and champagne light sources, creating depth through backdrop filters, specular rim lighting, and luminous edge highlights.
- **Editorial Poise**: Generous negative space, calibrated typographic contrast, and deliberate pacing mirror high-end horology, bespoke tailoring, and private aviation clienteling portals.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the sensory world of rare coffee terroir, noble metals, and nocturnal roastery architecture. The default mode is firmly dark, anchored by ink-black espresso tones to establish an intimate, cinematic backdrop.

### Key Roles
- **Primary (`#D4AF37` Champagne Gold)**: Represents prestige, distillation, and reserve-tier curation. Employed for key focal points, active states, premium badges, and fine hairline borders. Accompanied by an ethereal highlight tint (`#E6CA65`) for specular reflections and micro-glows.
- **Secondary (`#006241` Emerald Forest)**: Honors the heritage roastery identity. Used selectively for high-touch ambient glows, provenance indicators, seasonal micro-lot highlights, and tactile confirmation states. Complemented by `#00754A` for luminous volumetric diffusion.
- **Tertiary (`#F3E9DD` Creamy Latte)**: A soft, warm parchment tone that prevents harsh digital starkness. Utilized for pristine typography, editorial pull-quotes, and high-legibility interface data against deep backdrops. Supported by an artisanal tone (`#D7C4B7`) for subtle micro-copy and metadata.
- **Neutral (`#0B0F0D` Espresso Black)**: The foundational canvas. An ultra-deep, warm obsidian hue infused with minute green-black undertones, eliminating sterile pure blacks in favor of an organic, roasted depth.

### Surface Tiers
- **Surface Canvas**: `#0B0F0D` (Deep Espresso Base)
- **Surface Elevated (Tier 1)**: `rgba(18, 24, 21, 0.75)` with a 1px boundary of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`
- **Surface Modal / Overlay (Tier 2)**: `rgba(24, 31, 28, 0.88)` backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` with an inner specular gradient of `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(212,175,55,0.2) 0%, rgba(0,98,65,0.05) 100%)`

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy relies on the tension between classical haute-couture editorial display and precise, modern culinary informatics.

- **Editorial Serif (`Playfair Display`)**: Governs all sensory storytelling, lot names, origin narratives, and section titles. Headlines employ relaxed leading with subtle negative tracking for an authoritative, timeless poise.
- **Geometric Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Manages technical brewing parameters, cupping notes, sensory dials, price indicators, and utilitarian labels. The modern letterforms guarantee crystal-clear legibility on dark glass surfaces.
- **Uppercase Tracking Rules**: All metadata tags, tasting labels, process identifiers (e.g., "ANAEROBIC NATURAL", "MICRO-LOT #042"), and navigational actions must use `label-sm` or `label-md` rendered in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.08em` to `0.16em`) to echo high-end spirits packaging and luxury boutique menus.

## Layout & Spacing

The architectural layout utilizes a disciplined 12-column asymmetric fluid grid on desktop, shifting to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

### Spacing Philosophy
Negative space is treated as an active luxury material. Generous page margins frame imagery and narrative text like gallery exhibitions, resisting density in favor of calm, deliberate discovery.

- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12-column grid, `margin: 4rem`, `gutter: 2rem`. Elements favor asymmetrical offsets (e.g., tasting notes occupying columns 2–6 while imagery occupies 7–12).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 6-column grid, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Multi-tier cupping modules reflow into balanced dual-column cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Full bleed edge-to-edge sensory imagery balanced with inset card overlays.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on conventional drop shadows. Instead, it utilizes **multidimensional liquid depth**, combining physical dark glassmorphism, tinted specular rims, and soft radial underglows.

### Elevation Architecture
1. **Tier 0 (Base Canvas)**: Deep `#0B0F0D` overlaid with a subtle 2% organic noise texture to reproduce the tactile sensation of heavy matte paper stock.
2. **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Trays)**: 
   - Background: `rgba(16, 22, 19, 0.65)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px) saturate(140%)`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)`
   - Subtle Drop Shadow: `0 16px 36px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`
3. **Tier 2 (Interactive Modals & Floats)**:
   - Background: `rgba(18, 25, 22, 0.85)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(28px) saturate(160%)`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`
   - Drop Shadow: `0 24px 60px -16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 32px -8px rgba(0, 98, 65, 0.25)`
4. **Liquid Luminescence**:
   - Hero modules and elevated tasting selections feature an underlying radial gradient: `radial-gradient(circle at 50% 0%, rgba(0, 98, 65, 0.22) 0%, rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08) 45%, transparent 75%)`.
   - Hover states generate an internal champagne top-rim glow: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(230, 202, 101, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, architectural curvature profile (`roundedness: 1` — Soft, 0.25rem baseline).

- **Base Controls & Inputs**: Form fields, micro-chips, and utility buttons carry a subtle `4px` radius (`rounded-sm`), delivering tailored precision.
- **Boutique Tasting Cards & Glass Trays**: Utilize an `8px` radius (`rounded-lg`), softening the edges while retaining structural sophistication.
- **Pill Exceptions**: Badges highlighting rarity (e.g., "LOT 09", "ESTATE RESERVE") and circular sensory dials are permitted full pill/circular radii (`9999px`) to create geometric counterpoints against rectangular layout blocks.

### Buttons
- **Primary Luxury Button**:
  - Background: Solid Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`) transitioning to `#E6CA65` on hover.
  - Text: Deep Espresso Black (`#0B0F0D`), `label-md`, uppercase, bold.
  - Border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2).
  - Hover: Subtle gold particle glow `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary Atelier Ghost**:
  - Background: Transparent with a 1px border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`.
  - Text: Creamy Latte (`#F3E9DD`), `label-md`, uppercase.
  - Hover: Background shifts to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)` with border brightening to `#D4AF37`.

### Tasting Cards & Lot Showcases
- Built using Tier 1 glassmorphic containers with 1px gold hairline borders.
- Header showcases estate origin and lot number in `label-sm` champagne gold.
- Coffee title styled in `headline-sm` (`Playfair Display`) creamy latte text.
- Cupping notes presented as subtle pill tags with tinted emerald borders (`rgba(0, 98, 65, 0.4)`).
- Visual hover: The container elevates by 4px, border shifts from 18% to 40% gold opacity, and an ambient emerald halo reveals behind the coffee photography.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `rgba(11, 15, 13, 0.6)`.
- Border: 1px solid `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`.
- Text: `#F3E9DD`, `body-md`.
- Placeholder: `#D7C4B7` at 40% opacity.
- Focus: Border illuminates in solid `#D4AF37` with an inner glow of `0 0 12px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`.

### Chips & Provenance Badges
- Compact capsules framed with 1px borders of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`.
- Background: `rgba(0, 98, 65, 0.15)` for estate origin, `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)` for roast tier.
- Typography: `label-sm`, tracking `0.14em`, uppercase `#F3E9DD`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base: Custom square (checkbox) or circle (radio) with 1px border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`.
- Selected State: Fills with deep forest emerald (`#006241`) displaying a precision champagne gold check or inner dot; framed by a glowing perimeter of `#D4AF37`.

### Lists & Ledger Menus
- Editorial ledger styling inspired by private members' clubs.
- List items separated by delicate hairline dividers (`1px solid rgba(243, 233, 221, 0.08)`).
- Item name in `body-lg` (`#F3E9DD`), brewing variable or vintage in `body-sm` (`#D7C4B7`), and price aligned flush right in `label-lg` champagne gold (`#D4AF37`).

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Flavors Wheel Radar Chart**: Micro-faceted vector web diagram rendered in gold hairline strokes with emerald gradient fills, mapping notes like black cherry, bergamot, and smoked cacao.
- **Roast Profile Timeline**: Linear step gauge tracking development time and first crack using glowing gold nodes along an emerald spine.
- **Private Sommelier Concierge Sheet**: Tier 2 bottom-sheet modal surfaced for bespoke brewing consultations, featuring warm parchment accenting and gold-embossed actions.
